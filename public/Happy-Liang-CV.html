Job Decomposer

Framework-level job → task → sub-task dispatch.

Core designer of the job-decomposer library and heartbeat dispatch loop. At the framework level, a job is split recursively into tasks and sub-tasks across N levels and dispatched over asynchronous messaging. The heartbeat loop keeps dispatching, respects queue limits, applies backpressure, and scales workers from configuration or available resources. The design supports scalability, observability, stability, and robustness under load.
